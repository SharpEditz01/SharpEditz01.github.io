Questions

How fast will I get my edit?

Short videos and single designs are usually ready in a few days. We confirm the timeline before we start.

What do you need from me?

Raw footage or assets, references you like, and any brand colours or logos. Rough is fine.

Can I ask for changes?

Yes. Each package includes revision rounds so the final result matches what you had in mind.

Which formats do you deliver?

Vertical, square and widescreen video, plus PNG, JPG, PDF and source files for designs.

How do I pay?

We share a quote first. Payment details are agreed on before work begins.
