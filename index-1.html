FAQ

Good to know

How fast will I get my work?

Short videos and single designs are usually ready in a few days. We confirm the timeline before we begin.

What do you need from me?

Raw footage or assets, references you like, and any brand colours or logos. Rough is fine.

Can I request changes?

Yes. Every package includes revision rounds so the result matches your vision.

Which formats do you deliver?

Vertical, square and widescreen video, plus PNG, JPG, PDF and source files for designs.

How does payment work?

We share a quote first and agree payment details before work starts.
